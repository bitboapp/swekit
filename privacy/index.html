Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

SweKit has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you. Your collection, photos and notes are stored on your device. We never receive them.

Who we are

SweKit ("we", "us") is an independent app. For any question about this policy or your data, contact info@swekit.app.

Data stored on your device

The shirts you add, their photos, notes, care history, wishlist items, your collector profile and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too, unless you have exported a backup.

Smart Scan and photos

When you scan a shirt, the photos are analysed on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks (text recognition and background removal). The photos are not sent to a server. SweKit asks for camera and photo library access only so you can take or choose shirt photos.

Catalog updates

To recognise shirts, SweKit uses a catalog of kits. The app checks a small version file at swekit.app and downloads a new catalog only when you tap Update. These requests contain no personal data from the app. Like every website, our hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the files and keep the service secure. We do not use this data to identify you.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. We do not see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which plan you have. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app. This ID is not linked to your name or email address. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Backups, sharing and exports

When you export a backup, a PDF catalog or an image, you decide where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive or a messaging app). Encrypted backups are protected by a password you choose. We cannot open them and cannot recover a forgotten password.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. Home Screen widgets read data from the app on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Email support

If you email us, we use your email address and the content of your message only to answer you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

Children

SweKit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing and to data portability. Because your collection never leaves your device, you can view, export and delete it in the app at any time. For anything else, contact info@swekit.app. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
